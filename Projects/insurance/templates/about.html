About Our Project

This Insurance Price Predictor helps you estimate your insurance premium costs based on key attributes like age, sex, BMI, children, smoking habits, and region. Our project uses machine learning algorithms trained on real-world data to make accurate predictions and guide users in making better financial decisions.

It promotes transparency in premium calculations and assists users in understanding the key factors affecting their insurance pricing. This tool encourages informed decision-making and reduces pricing ambiguity in the insurance market.
